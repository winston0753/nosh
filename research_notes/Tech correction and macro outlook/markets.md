## What actually corrected and when?

### Takeaway
October's decline is predominantly a technology/AI repricing, with emerging credit spillovers. It is not yet broad-market proof of recession. Use October 30 completed closes as the stable market cutoff; November 2 figures encountered are intraday. Sources genuinely supply October/November 2026 data. The October 2026 episode is distinct from the March 2026 Iran-war correction.

## Are earnings collapsing or are cash returns deteriorating?

### Takeaway
The core distinction is strong sales/operating growth alongside sharply worsening cash generation after infrastructure spending. AI demand is demonstrable, but good demand does not establish adequate shareholder returns on incremental investment.

### Inferences
- Repricing is not purely empty sentiment: negative cash conversion and need for financing change equity risk, even if accounting earnings remain positive. Supplier revenue is partly another firm's capex; do not add cloud/AI supplier revenues and hyperscaler spending as independent final-demand pools.
- Conversely, claims that all AI demand is fictional or all technology earnings collapsed are contradicted by filings. Cloud revenues also include nonAI workloads and sometimes hardware sales, so they cannot all be labeled AI monetization.
- Broad forwardPE below averages weakens blanket bubble claims but does not make tech cheap or guarantee a floor: earnings forecasts can fall, sector weights change, and higher rates justify lower multiples.

## Do credit and breadth corroborate a broad downturn?

### Takeaway
Credit confirms an AI-financing warning, with some broad spread widening, but the available breadth/earnings evidence does not establish a systemwide contraction.

### Cited Findings
- ICE BofA US HY OAS on FRED: October30 3.44%=344bp; Oct29 3.26%; Oct28 3.14%; Oct27 3.18%; Oct26 3.30%. Latest page update November2 9:06AM CST. This is additional yield over an adjusted Treasury curve, not total corporate yield or default probability. [FRED primary distributor](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/BAMLH0A0HYM2).
- Reuters October15: Oracle5yearCDS288bp (+26bp overnight); SoftBank424bp (+24); CoreWeave over1000bp, citing S&P Global/LSEG. These are October15 observations, not current November2 levels. [Reuters October15 syndicated](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/ai-credit-default-swap-spreads-widen-sharply-ce785dd3d980f124).
- Reuters October30: declining/advancing ratios1.8:1NYSE and3.03:1Nasdaq; Nasdaq355newlows vs70newhighs, while S&P43newhighs vs5newlows; exchange volume19.87B vs20sessionaverage17.26B. [Reuters October30 closing report](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/nasdaq-futures-tumble-as-amazon-intel-results-deepen-ai-spending-woes-4986163).
- AP October29: majority stocks rose despite S&P falling0.3%; Russell gained1%. [AP October29](https://apnews.com/article/2c26b0b1bbc3bfab9f3f4b902172f458).

### Inferences
- Broad HY widened30bp fromOct28 to30, confirming some spillover. A single344bp observation and two-day change cannot establish recession; need persistent crosssector widening and underwriting/lending deterioration.
- Very wide individual AI CDS prices warrant concern but cannot be converted directly to default probabilities; spreads also depend on recovery, liquidity and hedging demand.
- October29 breadth divergence and October30 broader selloff show alternating rotation and contagion. Neither one session's advancers nor decliners proves a durable trend. Earnings and monthly index dispersion remain important counterevidence.
